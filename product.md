# Phi — Product

Phi is the superbrain for your investments, in your texts. Users connect a portfolio (read-only) and text Phi to **ask** (research across filings, earnings, news, market data, X, Reddit), **watch** (proactive alerts when something matters), and **simulate** (persistent simulated portfolios, clones, rebalances/hedges, copying investors).

**This repo (`phi-landing`) is the marketing landing page** — not the product itself. The actual product (backend, agent, infra) lives in the separate `phi-agent` repo.
